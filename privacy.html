CrimeStat

Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026.

CrimeStat is a free website you can also install on your phone’s home screen. This page describes what the site stores on your device and what it sends over the network. It does not invent practices that are not in the current site.

Stored on your device

CrimeStat uses your browser’s localStorage only. It does not create an account, and it does not use a CrimeStat login.

  • Your light or dark theme choice, if you set one in the header toggle.
  • Your last map preferences: time window, pin or heat view, and which crime layers are turned on.
  • Whether you dismissed the “Add to Home Screen” hint, so that hint can stay hidden.

You can clear this information by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Location

If you tap “Nearby me,” your browser may ask for permission to use your location. If you have already allowed location access, the map may use that location when it first loads, to center a 50-mile view and show a list of nearby incidents. That location is used only in your browser. CrimeStat does not send it to its servers and does not store it.

If you search an address, ZIP code, or city, that search text is sent to CrimeStat’s address lookup, which forwards the query to OpenStreetMap Nominatim so the map can be placed. Nominatim’s use of that query is governed by OpenStreetMap’s own practices.

Alerts form

The “Sign up for alerts” form is a preview only. Submitting it does not send or store anything on a server.

Map data and third-party services

The crime information on the map is official public data for the areas that have a feed. It is shown as of the date on the site and is not a real-time feed. For Auburn, pin popups show the official street addresses published by Auburn Police Department. In other covered cities, locations are shown at neighborhood, 100-block, or city level only. Rape / sexual assault, domestic violence, and stalking are area totals only, never pins or addresses.

To display the map, your browser also requests:

  • Map tiles from OpenStreetMap, Esri, or OpenTopoMap, depending on the base map you select. Those providers receive ordinary tile requests, which typically include your IP address and the map area being viewed.
  • The Leaflet map libraries from unpkg.
  • The Source Sans 3 font from Google Fonts.

Those services have their own terms and privacy policies.

Contact

CrimeStat does not run a user account system. For the live site, use the website itself. This page will be updated if these practices change.
