About this data

CrimeStat’s first release uses clearly labeled demo data. The points and area totals you see are simulated around the map center so the product can be tried. They are not police reports and should not be used for safety decisions.

When real feeds are connected, they will come from public sources such as city open-data portals or the FBI Crime Data Explorer, and sensitive types will stay at area level only.
